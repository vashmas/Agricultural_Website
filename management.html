Our Leadership

Meet the Management Team of Agrinova

Agrinova Group of Companies has been led by a dedicated and experienced management team since 1999. Our leadership combines deep knowledge of Pakistan's agricultural sector with a strong commitment to quality, innovation, and farmer support across all provinces of Pakistan.

Muhammad Shafiq Shahid

Chief Executive Officer

Muhammad Shafiq Shahid is the founder and Chief Executive Officer of Agrinova Group of Companies, Pakistan. With over 25 years of hands-on experience in Pakistan's agricultural industry, he established Agrinova in 1999 with a vision to provide high-quality crop protection products — including insecticides, fungicides, weedicides, and fertilizers — to farmers across Pakistan. Under his strategic leadership, Agrinova has grown into a nationwide brand with 200+ registered products and a trusted presence in all provinces of Pakistan.

Muhammad Saad Shafiq

Director Manager

Muhammad Saad Shafiq serves as Director Manager at Agrinova Group of Companies, overseeing daily business operations, departmental coordination, and service delivery across Pakistan. His focus on operational efficiency, quality control, and strong farmer relations ensures that Agrinova's 200+ agricultural products reach growers across all provinces of Pakistan with reliability and consistency.

Muhammad Behzad Shafiq

Director Finance

Muhammad Behzad Shafiq serves as Director Finance at Agrinova Group of Companies, managing all financial planning, budgeting, and fiscal operations. His expertise in agricultural business finance ensures the sound financial health, sustainable growth, and long-term economic stability of Agrinova across Pakistan. His strategic financial oversight supports the company's mission of expanding quality crop protection solutions nationwide.
