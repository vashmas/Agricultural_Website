Who We Are

Agrinova Group of Companies

Established in 1999, Agrinova Group of Companies is one of Pakistan's most trusted agricultural solutions providers. For over 25 years, we have been delivering high-quality crop protection products — insecticides, fungicides, weedicides, fertilizers, and granules — to farmers across every province of Pakistan.

Our mission is simple: empower Pakistani farmers with reliable, affordable, and effective agricultural inputs that boost crop yields, protect fields, and ensure food security for generations to come.

Nationwide Presence

From Punjab to Sindh, KPK to Balochistan — Agrinova serves farmers all over Pakistan through an extensive distribution network of dealers and agricultural consultants at the field level.

200+ Quality Products

With over 200 registered agricultural products, our range covers complete crop protection — from bollworm and whitefly control to blight management, weed suppression, and soil nutrition.

25 Years of Trust
